Who processes data

AdPilot is operated by HABELEV STANISLAV. Contact: psbig@projectstudio-big.net or @sgabelev on Telegram.

Data we use

Telegram ID and public profile data; interview and onboarding answers; project settings; OAuth tokens and connected Meta/TikTok account identifiers; campaign data and metrics; support requests; free strategic review and subscription status; security logs.

To provide accounts, prepare and launch campaigns at your request, retrieve metrics, create reports, provide recommendations, process payments, support users and prevent abuse.

Security and retention

Secrets and tokens should be stored with restricted access. Data is kept for as long as necessary to provide the service, meet obligations and protect legitimate interests, then deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion, restriction of processing, and revoke platform access. Some information may be retained where required by law or necessary to resolve a dispute.
